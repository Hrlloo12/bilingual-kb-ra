# Qimam Home Gift Cards

## Gift card values

Qimam Home gift cards are sold in values of 100, 250, 500 and 1,000 SAR.

## Validity

A gift card is valid for 24 months from the date of purchase. The balance can be spent over several purchases until it reaches zero.

## Where gift cards can be used

Gift cards can be used in every Qimam Home showroom and in the online store. They cannot be used to pay delivery or installation fees.

## Lost cards and refunds

Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash. A lost gift card can be replaced once, if the customer shows the original purchase receipt.
