# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

## Submitting Claims

Expense claims are submitted through the finance portal with a short description of the business purpose.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Original receipts are required for any expense above 100 SAR.

## Personal Car Use

Use of a personal car for business trips is reimbursed at 0.75 SAR per kilometre.

## Flights

Flights shorter than 6 hours are booked in economy class for all grades below G7. Flights are booked through the corporate travel desk.

## Corporate Cards

Corporate cards are issued to employees in grade G5 and above. Card statements must be reconciled every month.
