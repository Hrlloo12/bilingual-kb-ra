# Returns and Refunds Summary

## Return Eligibility

Products can be returned within 14 days of delivery if they are unused and in their original packaging.

Custom-made and made-to-measure products cannot be returned. Mattresses cannot be returned once the protective hygiene seal has been removed.

## Return Collection

A collection appointment is arranged after the return request is approved.

Returns of non-defective items are subject to a 99 SAR collection fee.

## Refunds

Approved refunds are processed within 7 business days after the returned item is inspected. Customers receive a confirmation message once the refund has been issued.
