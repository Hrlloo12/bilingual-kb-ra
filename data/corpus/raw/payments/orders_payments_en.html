Orders and Payments Guide

Payment Options

Accepted payment methods are mada, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, bank transfer and cash on delivery.

Orders above 2,000 SAR can be paid in 4 interest-free monthly installments.

Tracking Your Order

Customers can track their order status on the website using the order number and mobile number.

Cancelling an Order

Cancelling an order after it has been dispatched incurs a fee of 10% of the order value.

Price Match Promise

Qimam Home matches the price of an identical product from an authorised Saudi retailer within 7 days of purchase. A copy of the competitor's quotation or invoice is required.
