Ticket Priorities and Escalation Procedure

Purpose

This procedure explains how support tickets are prioritised and how unresolved critical issues are escalated inside the Qimam Home support organisation.

Ticket Priorities

  • Priority 1 (critical): the customer cannot use a delivered product at all, or there is a safety risk.
  • Priority 2 (high): a product can be used but a major function is affected.
  • Priority 3 (normal): general questions, cosmetic issues and information requests.

Escalation of Critical Tickets

Priority 1 (critical) tickets not resolved within 2 hours are escalated to the duty support manager.

The duty support manager reviews the ticket, assigns additional technicians if required and keeps the customer informed until the issue is closed.

Requesting an Escalation

Customers can ask for an escalation by replying to the ticket and explaining why the issue is urgent. The support agent records the request and informs the team leader.
