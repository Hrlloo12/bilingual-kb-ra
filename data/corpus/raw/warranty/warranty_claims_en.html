Warranty Coverage and Claims Guide

Warranty Periods

Warranty coverage starts on the delivery date recorded on the order.

Bedroom furniture carries a 5-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Kitchens carry a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects.

The gas lift and tilt mechanism of office chairs are covered by the warranty for 7 years. Mattresses are covered for 10 years against sagging deeper than 3 cm.

Extended Warranty

An extended warranty adding 2 years can be purchased for 399 SAR within 30 days of delivery. The extended warranty applies to the same components as the standard warranty.

Claim Process

Customers submit a claim with photos of the defect through the customer portal. The service team reviews the claim and contacts the customer to confirm the details.

After a warranty claim is approved, a technician visit is scheduled within 5 business days.

What Is Not Covered

The warranty does not cover misuse, water or moisture damage, or damage caused by self-assembly.
