# Dining Product Specifications

## About This Sheet

This sheet lists the overall dimensions of each product in the Dining range. Dimensions are given in centimetres as width × depth × height.

## Waha dining table (QH-DN-501)

The Waha dining table (code QH-DN-501) measures 180 × 90 × 76 cm (width × depth × height).

## Sidr dining chair (QH-DN-502)

The Sidr dining chair (code QH-DN-502) measures 48 × 55 × 98 cm (width × depth × height).

## Ghaith extendable dining table (QH-DN-503)

The Ghaith extendable dining table (code QH-DN-503) measures 160 × 95 × 76 cm (width × depth × height).

## Mira round table (QH-DN-504)

The Mira round table (code QH-DN-504) measures 120 × 120 × 76 cm (width × depth × height).

## Areej buffet (QH-DN-511)

The Areej buffet (code QH-DN-511) measures 160 × 45 × 85 cm (width × depth × height).

## Jumana bench (QH-DN-512)

The Jumana bench (code QH-DN-512) measures 140 × 40 × 46 cm (width × depth × height).
